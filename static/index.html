Understanding Pretrained Vision Models

How Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) & Transfer Learning identify visual patterns.

01

What is a Pretrained Model?

Training an AI vision model from zero requires millions of categorized images and weeks of GPU compute. A pretrained model (like MobileNetV2 or ResNet) has already learned visual representations on the ImageNet-1K dataset (1.4M images across 1,000 object categories).

02

Hierarchical Feature Learning

Early Layers: Detect raw edges, line orientations, and color transitions.
Middle Layers: Combine edges to detect shapes, curves, and textures (e.g. fur, wheels).
Deep Layers: Synthesize shapes into semantic objects (e.g. dog ears, eyes, car grilles).

03

Preprocessing Pipeline

Before entering the model, every image is:
1. Resized & Center-Cropped to 224 × 224.
2. Converted to tensor values in range [0, 1].
3. Normalized with ImageNet mean `[0.485, 0.456, 0.406]` and std `[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]`.

04

Grad-CAM Explainability

Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) calculates gradients of the predicted class with respect to the final convolutional feature maps. It creates a heat map showing the exact pixel regions that triggered the model's decision!
